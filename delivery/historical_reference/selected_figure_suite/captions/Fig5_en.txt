Figure 5 | Probability reliability and validation-selection behavior.

A,B, All 120 saved calibration-bin summaries for LR (graphite), CatBoost (ochre) and TabICL (wine), covering internal repeated-test pooling and temporal validation at six and eight years. The horizontal coordinate is the saved mean predicted risk, rather than the bin number; the vertical coordinate is observed fraction minus mean prediction. Open squares denote internal and filled circles temporal validation. Marker area is an affine function of each bin's share within its own model/setting/horizon curve; internal repeated records are not interpreted as distinct participants. The horizontal reference denotes zero calibration residual, and bin-level uncertainty intervals are unavailable in the saved outputs. C,D, Six- and eight-year paired net-benefit differences for TabICL minus LR (cobalt) and CatBoost (vermilion), scaled per 1,000 participants. Shading shows pointwise percentile 95% CIs from 2,000 shared household-cluster bootstrap samples retaining all household members. Every comparison retains all 79 fixed thresholds from 0.01 to 0.40, without smoothing or outcome-based threshold selection. Band opacity is 24%. These intervals condition on saved local fitted models, exclude training variability and do not provide simultaneous threshold coverage. A,B,E,F retain the original author summaries and are not from the same prediction runs as C,D. E, Empirical distributions of fit time across all 680 saved validation-search runs, pooled over both horizons: LR 240, CatBoost 360 and TabICL 80 runs. The horizontal axis is logarithmic. F, All 60 saved repeat-specific validation thresholds; filled up-triangles denote six years and open down-triangles eight years, with short median bars. Small horizontal offsets separate repeats and do not encode performance. These saved-curve and validation-selection diagnostics are descriptive and do not establish clinical utility. Overall discrimination and numerical model-performance comparisons remain in the accompanying tables.
