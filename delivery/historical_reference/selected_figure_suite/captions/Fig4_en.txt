Figure 4 | A common predictive core alongside model-specific importance.

A, Permutation-importance ranks of all 60 original predictors across LR, GNB, DT, LightGBM, CatBoost, RealMLP and TabICL in the saved six-year analysis. Age, education and map-use difficulty are highlighted; all other variables remain visible in gray. B, Median rank and interquartile rank range across the same seven models, with marker area increasing with the number of models placing the variable in the top ten. Model counts are descriptive agreement measures, not independent experimental replicates. C, All 300 paired permutation and mean-absolute-SHAP ranks from the five models with saved SHAP summaries. Solid and dashed contours enclose 50% and 80% of a reflected Gaussian rank-density estimate over the bounded rank domain; they are not confidence intervals. The dotted line indicates rank agreement. D, All 420 saved permutation-importance point estimates, displayed by within-model rank on a symmetric-logarithmic importance scale; age, education and map use retain their highlight colors. No confidence intervals are shown. Only the seven models satisfying the stored prediction-reconstruction criterion enter the primary consensus; the original GAT record remains in the source audit. Importance denotes loss of AUPRC after permutation, rather than an effect direction, causal contribution or clinical intervention effect. Cross-model absolute SHAP magnitudes are not compared. No new model fit or individual explanation is generated.
