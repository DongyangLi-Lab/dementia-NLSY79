Figure 1 | Observation windows and the age distribution of first detected dementia.

A,B, Complete observed-state paths across baseline and the 2-, 4- and 6-year survey waves in the 2000 development cohort (n = 13,658) and 2010 temporal cohort (n = 14,659). Ribbon widths represent participant counts. N denotes observed non-dementia, D observed dementia and ? an unavailable assessment; ? is not assigned a death status. Color distinguishes paths with an observed dementia assessment (wine), fully assessed non-dementia paths (purple), and other paths (gray). State changes describe survey classifications rather than irreversible clinical transitions. C,D, Stacked two-year baseline-age histograms of the first wave at which dementia was observed, accounting for all 1,593 and 1,699 observed cases, respectively. Colors indicate first detection at years 2, 4 or 6. E, Participant counts for every observed state-sequence pattern, ordered separately within each baseline cohort; both axes are logarithmic. F, Cumulative observed-case share when participants are ordered from oldest to youngest. Filled areas indicate the difference from the identity reference and are not confidence intervals. First detection is survey-wave detection rather than an exact onset time. All panels describe the selected, unweighted analytic cohorts; no survival model or new hypothesis test is introduced.
